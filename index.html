02The series

Same car.
Skill decides.

The Collegiate Racing Series is a national championship for university teams. Every team races an identically prepared Mazda MX-5 Miata, so no one can buy their way to the front.

Results come down to preparation, strategy and driving. Through the series' partnership with IMSA, the top sports car racing organization in North America, our members also get a direct line to motorsport internships and careers.

07Questions

Good
to know.

Is my contribution tax-deductible?

We are setting up our 501(c)(3) nonprofit now. Once it is approved, gifts to the team can be tax-deductible. Ask us for the latest status before you give.

Is racing safe?

Races are held at professional, sanctioned tracks under series safety rules, with race-prepared cars and licensed drivers. The Collegiate Racing Series carries liability insurance for every participant at every event.

Can I help with products instead of money?

Yes. Trailers, truck rentals, tools, shop space, printing, food and professional services all make a real difference, and in-kind partners are recognized just like financial partners.

Are there brands you can't carry?

Series rules do not allow alcohol, tobacco, gambling or adult brands on the car. Restaurants and bars are welcome to partner as restaurants.

When and where does the team race?

Our first season opens in February 2027 at professional racetracks around the country. The Collegiate Racing Series will announce the full calendar.
